A charming little game about a retired frog (or at least, I _think_ he's retired), who spends his days trimming the grass around his cozy house and relaxing in between. Every now and then, he waves at you and cheers you on with lines like _“You’ve been working hard today!”_ or _“One step at a time...”_ He’s like a comforting companion, helping you ease into a productive mindset. I mean, I can’t just sit idle while my little "colleague" is out there diligently mowing grass on the second screen.

That’s exactly what I often miss in lo-fi productivity games — just a touch of interactivity. Someone rustling through notebook pages, typing thoughtfully, or humming softly on the other side of the screen. In that sense, I’m still very much looking forward to _Chill with You: Lo-Fi Story_ — but _Ropuka’s Idle Island_ is doing a great job of keeping me company while I wait.

You can use the trimmed grass to either upgrade your frog’s skills (so he mows faster and more efficiently), or trade it for coins. Coins, in turn, are spent on cosmetic upgrades for the frog’s yard — a new house, fences, a cozy chair, and so on.

What’s important is that the game never pressures you. You can leave your little grass-cutter alone for the entire day and come back in the evening to spend all the accumulated resources. Or check in every hour. Or even forget about it for days — the grass will still be there, and there’s no storage limit.

The game also comes with a set of six ambient sounds and three lo-fi music tracks. You can mix and match them or turn them off completely. Personally, I often leave the ambient on and mute the music — the tracks are nice, but they get repetitive quickly.

The island itself floats above all your windows, and you can resize or reposition it however you like. The main thing is to make sure both you and the frog feel cozy in each other’s company. ♥
